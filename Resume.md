# Selected Engineering Projects

## IMU-Based Manufacturing Fixture
**Manufacturing Engineering | Sensors | Precision Fixture**

**Problem:** Repeated rework occurred because the top-plate shaft could jam in the chamber hole, creating assembly delays and line-down risk.

**Engineering approach:** Ideated, designed and developed an IMU-based fixture that measured the absolute position of the top plate and chamber to quantify planar misalignment. The solution was exercised through multiple real assemblies by production technicians, followed by measured time and labour-hour comparison.

**Result:** **90% cycle-time reduction, more than $1M annual savings, and elimination of line-down events caused by the jam condition.**

## Automated Hardware Validation
**Python | Hardware Test Automation | Validation**

**Problem:** Repetitive manual hardware validation consumed engineering time and limited throughput.

**Engineering approach:** Developed a Python-based testing system to automate hardware validation workflows.

**Result:** **50% reduction in test cycles** with support for SEMI S2/S8 compliance-oriented testing.

## Assembly Workflow Simulation
**DELMIA | Manufacturing Simulation | Process Engineering**

**Problem:** Assembly planning relied heavily on physical trial and error.

**Engineering approach:** Simulated assembly workflows before physical execution to identify process issues earlier.

**Result:** **30% reduction in trial-and-error time.**

## Critical Mobile X-Ray Supplier Transition
**Medical Devices | Supplier Engineering | V&V**

**Problem:** A manufacturing shortage threatened the customer commissioning timeline for critical mobile X-ray components.

**Engineering approach:** Worked through supplier prototypes, assembly guidance, documentation, V&V and risk retirement, and designed a fixture-based strategy to defer a constrained assembly operation to field commissioning.
